# Privacy Review

Use this skill for policy, education, children, healthcare, public-sector, or user-facing AI projects.

## Workflow

1. Inventory personal, sensitive, child-related, operational, and derived data.
2. Trace where data enters prompts, logs, caches, memory, analytics, exports, and third-party APIs.
3. Check whether data is minimized, transformed, retained, or shared unnecessarily.
4. Verify secrets and credentials are not stored in prompts, memory, docs, or bootstrap metadata.
5. Identify consent, retention, access, and deletion assumptions.
6. Summarize privacy risks and concrete mitigations.

## Report Shape

- Data categories
- Data flow summary
- Prompt/log/memory exposure points
- Retention assumptions
- Third-party exposure
- Recommended mitigations
- Open privacy questions
